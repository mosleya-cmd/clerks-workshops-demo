Developed with MoCCFOA

WashU's School of Continuing & Professional Studies is the education partner of the Missouri City Clerks and Finance Officers Association (MoCCFOA). Workshops are planned with association members and built around real scenarios from Missouri municipalities.

Questions before you register

Do I need a Zoom account?

No. You can join from the free Zoom app or a web browser on a computer or phone.

How do I get my continuing education points?

Each workshop earns clerks 1 CE point. Attend live, then complete the short assessment [on the MoCCFOA website]. MoCCFOA records your points.

Can several people from my office attend?

Yes. Each person registers with their own email, so each gets a personal Zoom link and their own CE credit.

Can my city pay by invoice or purchase order?

Checkout takes credit and debit cards. [If your city needs to pay by invoice or purchase order, contact us before you register.]

What if I can't attend live?

CE points require live attendance. [Workshops are recorded. Whether recordings are offered afterward, and on what terms, is to confirm.]
